House rules

  • Stored prices are tax-inclusive: “Price includes applicable tax”. Tax is never added again — screen, list, API and PDF show the same amount.
  • Only approved payment proofs can be attached to an invoice; one proof per invoice.
  • No student email addresses are collected or invented; no unnecessary accounts are created.
  • Meeting links and notifications require a working authorized integration, so none are fabricated.
