# Operating rules — Clearpath Coding Academy
Fixture MINED-T428-V3. Roles: administrator, staff, faculty and student. Enforce authorization in pages and API routes.
Staff may type a student and program, attach authorized payment proof, generate an invoice and download a branded PDF. Stored prices are tax-inclusive; never add tax again. Do not invent student email addresses or create unnecessary accounts. Generated billing email is omitted.
Administrators manage role and invoice records. Faculty manage course/class scheduling, materials and authorized sharing. Students see only enrolled learning and their invoice information. Meeting links and notifications require a working authorized integration; no plausible fabricated URLs.
This repository is a prerequisite baseline. PDF generation, upload processing, role controls and the complete portal remain unimplemented.
